# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and constructive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Keep criticism focused on the work.
- Respect privacy when discussing transcripts, logs, local files, and generated fragments.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing transcripts, logs, or generated notes that contain secrets or personal data.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or maintainer work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other reasonable action to protect the project community.
